About Us

Supporting Farmers From Seedling to Successful Production

Wisani Agri Solutions focuses on vegetable seedling production, while also providing agricultural mentorship and coaching for aspiring and existing growers.

Led by Farmer and Director Wisani Mapapila, the business connects quality seedlings with practical, hands-on farming guidance — helping growers build productive and sustainable operations.

Vegetable Seedling Production

Quality seedlings produced for farmers and growers who need healthy plants for their production cycles.

Agricultural Mentorship

Practical guidance for people who want to understand farming and improve their agricultural knowledge.

Farm Coaching

Hands-on support that connects agricultural knowledge with real farming operations.

Farm Visits

Practical exposure to farming operations, nursery infrastructure and production processes.
